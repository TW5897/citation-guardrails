# Introduction

Low-dose colchicine reduced the composite of cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, ischemic stroke or ischemia-driven revascularization by 31% in patients with chronic coronary disease [1]. Colchicine also reduced all-cause mortality in this population [1]. After myocardial infarction, colchicine lowered the risk of ischemic cardiovascular events (HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.61-0.96) [2]. Endocardial fibroelastosis is associated with acute ischemic stroke in adults [3]. Hydroxychloroquine was associated with increased in-hospital mortality in COVID-19 [4]. The benefit of colchicine was consistent across prior acute coronary syndrome status [7].

# Methods

We followed PRISMA 2020 guidance [5, 6]. Fig. 1 shows the flow. Our own cohort had a 4.0-percentage-point lower readmission rate (own data).

# Results

In our analysis, 12 of 141 patients (8.5%) had events.
